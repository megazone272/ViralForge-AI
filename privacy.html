Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Overview

ViralForge AI is a content automation project. This policy explains how information may be handled when users use the service.

Information we may process

Depending on enabled features, the service may process account information, project content, generated media, publishing metadata, and authorized social-platform account information.

Social platform data

When a user connects a supported social account, ViralForge uses only the permissions authorized by that user and only to provide the requested integration, such as publishing content and retrieving permitted status or analytics.

Security

Credentials and tokens should be stored securely on the server and must not be exposed to the browser or public source code.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information or social-platform data. Data may be processed by service providers required to operate enabled AI, storage, rendering, or platform integrations.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use GitHub Issues.
